Notification Bridge forwards your smartphone's notifications to a second device over Bluetooth. Each notification is sent as a small text file, or as a vMessage text message for phones that understand it, using OBEX Object Push, the file-transfer profile that basic phones have supported for twenty years. The receiving phone needs no app, no internet and no setup beyond pairing.

Useful if you want to carry a simpler phone, save battery or disconnect, without losing sight of your WhatsApp, Telegram or email notifications and incoming calls.

<b>Features</b>
• Forwards notifications from the apps you choose, plus incoming calls (native or VoIP).
• Skips apps you haven't selected, silent and ongoing notifications, and reposts.
• Limits each app to 10 transfers per minute; optional batching of a conversation into one file.
• Retries failed transfers and pauses after repeated failures.
• Dumbphone mode for unattended use: minimal status notification, automatic reconnection, alert only when a transfer fails for good.
• Readiness checklist, transfer history that explains failures, and a diagnostics report you can copy.
• Configuration export and import, custom colour themes, light and dark mode.
• 11 languages.

<b>Privacy</b>
• No Internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
• Notification text is sent only to the Bluetooth receiver you choose. The app does not encrypt it and does not hide one-time codes, so only allow apps whose notifications you are comfortable having on the receiver.
• Notification content is never written to disk; history keeps only the app name and a short title, in memory.
• The bridge is off until you turn it on.

<b>Good to know</b>
• Needs Notification access, which you grant in the system settings.
• Tested so far with an Alcatel 3080A. Other phones that accept Bluetooth OBEX Object Push should work.
• Do not rely on it as your only way to receive urgent or safety-critical communications.
